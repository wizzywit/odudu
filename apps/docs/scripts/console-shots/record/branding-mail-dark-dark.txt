What the mail inherits
Mail takes the tenant’s published logo and colours, and its message overrides. A mail whose flow began inside a client’s sign-in takes that client’s logo and colours over these. This shows the tenant’s own; choose a client to see the mail its sign-in sends. Never a stylesheet or a template. Where the theme sets nothing, the mail keeps Odudu’s own colours and mark.
A mail client chooses the light or the dark colours by its own setting, so each is listed here and the preview shows one at a time.
