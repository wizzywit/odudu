Before you start
Your messages, and the tenant data the assistant reads to answer them, are sent to Anthropic.
You can read it again later. People who can read your own tenant’s audit trail can read it too, unless an administrator there has turned that off in Settings.
Your own tenant pays for this conversation. Administrators of demo see that you used the assistant there and which tools it ran, not what you wrote or what it answered.
