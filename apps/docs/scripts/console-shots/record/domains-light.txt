Domains
The addresses this tenant can sign in at, and the one it uses now. A tenant signs in at its path address until one of its domains is made the address.
Register a domain
Register a host you control, publish the TXT record Odudu gives you for it, then verify it. Only a verified domain can become the tenant’s address. A pending domain that is never verified is removed after a few days.
Host
A lowercase name such as login.acme.com, without a scheme, path or port.

Switching the address
A switch signs everyone in at the tenant out, because a session belongs to the address it began at, and moves the issuer, the sign-in pages and the links in mail.
For 1 hour after a switch the previous address keeps serving the token, userinfo, introspection, revocation, key and logout endpoints, so what was already issued keeps working. It refuses sign-in, registration and password-reset pages, and points to the new address. After that it serves nothing for this tenant.
Passkeys belong to the address they were registered at, so a switch hides the ones registered at the other address, and a switch back brings them back. Before a switch the console counts the subjects it would affect.
Switching back to the path address is always possible, whatever state a domain is in.
