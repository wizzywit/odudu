# System administrators in the console

Most administrators run one tenant. A few have to run them all: create tenants, look into any of them, recover one that has lost its own administrators. Those are the system administrators, the people of the `system` tenant who hold the capability to reach every other. This page shows who they are and changes the list. It needs the `manage-tenants` capability; the menu entry is **System administrators** under **SYSTEM**, and only an administrator of the system tenant sees it.

## 1. See who holds what

Open **System administrators**. It lists everybody in `system` who holds an admin capability, with how each holds it: directly, through a group or nested in another role. Use **Filter administrators** to search by **Username**, or filter by **Status** and by what they hold under **Holds**. An administrator marked "reaches every tenant" holds `manage-tenants`, which **Full (tenant-admin)** carries here. Anything less acts in the system tenant only.

## 2. Change what one holds

Choose the button on a row that is named for the person, such as **Change admin@odudu.local’s capabilities**. Pick what they should hold and save. A change that would leave the system tenant with no enabled holder of `manage-tenants` is refused, so you can never lock everybody out by accident.

## 3. Add an administrator

Under **Add an administrator**, find a subject of the system tenant in **Subject in system**, choose what they should hold under **What they hold** (you can give only what you hold yourself), and choose **Give it to the chosen subject**. If they do not exist yet, choose **Create a new subject as an administrator**. The console guides you through creating them and shows a one-time password once, which they change at their first sign-in. Nobody is given a password here.

## Things that cannot be undone, or that are easy to get wrong

- **Full (tenant-admin)** in the system tenant is the keys to every tenant: every person, client, key and audit trail. Give it sparingly, and give a narrower capability, such as `view-audit`, to somebody who only needs to look.
- Taking `manage-tenants` away from the last enabled holder is refused. Make a second administrator first.
- Disabling or deleting an administrator under [Subjects](./subjects.mdx) takes their reach with it, and is subject to the same refusal.

## Do the same through the API

Everything here is done with the [subjects](../reference/generated/admin-api/subjects/index.md) and [subject access](../reference/generated/admin-api/subject-access/index.md) areas of the admin API in the `system` tenant: create the subject, then assign it an admin capability. The first administrator of an installation is made when you [start Odudu](../quick-start/index.mdx).
