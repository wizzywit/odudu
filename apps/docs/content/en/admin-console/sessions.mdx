# Sessions in the console

A session is the record that somebody is signed in. It is what keeps a person from typing their password at every page, and it is what you end when a laptop is lost, an employee leaves or you want everybody to sign in again. This page is for seeing who is signed in and for ending it. It needs the `manage-sessions` capability; the menu entry is **Sessions** under **SECURITY**.

## 1. See who is signed in

Open **Sessions**. It lists every browser sign-in in the tenant that has neither ended nor idled out, whoever holds it, a page at a time. Each row names the subject, the clients the session reached and when it started and was last active. **Device** is the browser the session was opened on. Each browser's sessions sit together on the page and say how many there are, sessions opened before browsers were recorded come last with a dash, and the copy button gives the whole value. Grouping applies to the rows loaded so far. The subject's name links to their record.

The administrator who signs in to the console is signed in to the tenant they chose at the start. A system administrator working in `demo` therefore appears under `system`, not here.

## 2. End one person's sessions

The list ends sessions for a whole tenant only. To end one person's, open them under [Subjects](./subjects.mdx), choose their **Sessions** tab and use **End session** on a row, or **End every session** to sign them out everywhere. Ending a session revokes the grants it holds and tells every client that registered a back-channel logout address.

## 3. End every session in the tenant

Choose **End every session** and read what the dialog says before you confirm. Every live session in the tenant ends, each person is signed out and each client with a back-channel logout address is told. At most five hundred end in one go, and the dialog tells you if more are left, in which case you choose it again.

Two things are left alone. A session whose person holds an admin capability you do not hold stays, so you cannot cut off someone more powerful than you. And a refresh token issued with `offline_access` is bound to no session, so it survives; cut those off from the person's **Grants** tab, or from the client's **Sessions** tab with **Revoke every token**.

## Things that cannot be undone

**End every session** cannot be undone. It signs everyone out at once, and people lose whatever they had not saved. If your own console session belongs to the tenant, it is one of them and the console signs you out at its next request.

## Do the same through the API

The calls are in the [sessions area of the admin API](../reference/generated/admin-api/sessions/index.md); one person's sessions are on the [subject sessions area](../reference/generated/admin-api/subject-sessions/index.md). To change how long a session lasts, see [Settings](./settings.mdx), and to understand the idle limit and the maximum lifetime read [Sessions](../concepts/sessions.mdx).
