# Sign-in flow in the console

When a person signs in, Odudu asks them for something, then perhaps for something more. What it asks, and in what order, is the tenant's sign-in flow. A new tenant starts with a sensible one: a passkey or a password, then a one-time password from an authenticator app for anyone who set one up. This page is for changing it. It needs the `manage-tenant` capability; the menu entry is **Sign-in flow** under **SECURITY**.

## 1. Read the steps

Under **Steps**, each row is one thing a person can be asked: a passkey, a password, an `otp` (a one-time password from an authenticator app) or a `recovery-code`. The steps run in the order shown, at every sign-in.

## 2. Choose how strictly each is asked

Each step has a requirement.

- **Required**: every sign-in must pass it.
- **Alternative**: any one of a run of alternatives that sit next to each other is enough. A passkey and a password side by side mean a person may use either.
- **Conditional**: asked only of a person it applies to, and skipped for the rest. A one-time password applies to someone who set up an authenticator app.
- **Disabled**: left in the flow but skipped.

To require a password every time, change **Requirement for password** to **Required**.

## 3. Change the order

Use **Up** and **Down** on a row to move a step. Two alternatives that end up apart are no longer one run, so check the order you leave behind.

## Add a step

A step that is not in the list can be added. Choose it under **Add a step**, choose its requirement under **Requirement of the new step**, then add it. It joins the end of the list.

## 4. Save the whole list

Choose **Save Steps**. The whole list is replaced at once, so nothing is half-changed. People already signed in are not asked again; the new flow applies to the next sign-in.

## 5. Ask everyone for a one-time password

The one-time password step is asked only of people who set up an authenticator app. To ask it of every person, including those who have not, turn on **Ask every subject for a one-time password** under **One-time password** and save. A person without an authenticator is then taken to set one up, and their sign-in does not finish until they have. A sign-in with a passkey is never asked again. With this on, a [password minimum](./settings.mdx#4-choose-a-password-policy) of 8 characters is enough.

## Passkeys

A passkey step does nothing for a person until they have registered a passkey. There is no tenant switch that asks everyone for one. Tick **Register a passkey** under **Required actions** on a person's record, or email them the link, and they register one in their browser at their next sign-in. A passkey is tied to the host name in the server's public address, `ODUDU_PUBLIC_BASE_URL`. If that host changes, passkeys made under the old one stop working. Browsers offer passkeys only on an https address, or on `localhost`.

## Things that cannot be undone, or that lock people out

Nothing here deletes data, but a flow can lock people out. Requiring a step that people have not set up, or disabling the only step they can pass, leaves them unable to sign in until you change it back. Test a change by signing in with a person of the tenant before you leave the page, and keep a second administrator session open while you do.
