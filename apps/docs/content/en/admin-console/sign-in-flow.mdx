# Sign-in flow in the console

When a person signs in, Odudu asks them for something, then perhaps for something more. What it asks, and in what order, is the tenant's sign-in flow. A new tenant starts with a sensible one: a passkey or a password, then a one-time password from an authenticator app for anyone who set one up. This page is for changing it. It needs the `manage-tenant` capability; the menu entry is **Sign-in flow** under **SECURITY**. A tenant that has a [domain of its own](../operating/custom-domains.md) signs its administrators in to this console through that domain, so they pass the same flow there.

## 1. Read the steps

Under **Steps**, each row is one thing a person can be asked: a passkey, a password, an `otp` (a one-time password from an authenticator app) or a `recovery-code`. The steps run in the order shown, at every sign-in.

## 2. Choose how strictly each is asked

Each step has a requirement.

- **Required**: every sign-in must pass it.
- **Alternative**: any one of a run of alternatives that sit next to each other is enough. A passkey and a password side by side mean a person may use either.
- **Conditional**: asked only of a person it applies to, and skipped for the rest. A one-time password applies to someone who set up an authenticator app.
- **Disabled**: left in the flow but skipped.

To require a password every time, change **Requirement for password** to **Required**.

## 3. Change the order

Use **Up** and **Down** on a row to move a step. Two alternatives that end up apart are no longer one run, so check the order you leave behind.

## Add a step

A step that is not in the list can be added. Choose it under **Add a step**, choose its requirement under **Requirement of the new step**, then add it. It joins the end of the list.

## 4. Save the whole list

Choose **Save Steps**. The whole list is replaced at once, so nothing is half-changed. People already signed in are not asked again; the new flow applies to the next sign-in.

## 5. Ask everyone for a one-time password

The one-time password step is asked only of people who set up an authenticator app. To ask it of every person, including those who have not, turn on **Ask every subject for a one-time password** under **One-time password** and save. A person without an authenticator is then taken to set one up, and their sign-in does not finish until they have. A sign-in with a passkey is never asked again. With this on, a [password minimum](./settings.mdx#4-choose-a-password-policy) of 8 characters is enough.

## Several flows

The steps above are those of the tenant's default flow, which every sign-in runs. A tenant can hold other flows beside it, at most 20 in all, each with a name of its own. **Flows**, under the one-time password setting, lists them by name with the default marked **Default** and the steps each holds in order. No client can name one yet, so a flow other than the default is not run by any sign-in until you make it the default; clients gain that choice in a later release.

- **New flow** asks for a name, unique within the tenant and at most 64 characters, and **Create flow** makes it with a copy of the default flow's steps. A name another flow holds, or a tenant already at 20 flows, is refused under the name.
- **Edit** opens the flow. Its name and its steps are changed together, in the same way as the steps above, and **Save Steps** replaces both at once. Another flow's name is refused with the name it belongs to. The default flow can be renamed and its steps changed here too; it stays the default.
- **Delete** removes a flow after you confirm. The default flow has no **Delete**: a sign-in that names no flow runs it, so make another flow the default first.
- **Make default** on any other row asks you to confirm, then makes that flow the tenant's default. The flow that was the default becomes an ordinary one, which can then be deleted. Every sign-in that names no flow runs the new default from then on, and a sign-in already under way takes its next step from it.

## Passkeys

A passkey step does nothing for a person until they have registered a passkey. To ask everyone for one, turn on **Require a passkey** under **Authenticators** in [Settings](./settings.mdx#asking-everyone-for-a-passkey). To ask one person, tick **Register a passkey** under **Required actions** on their record, or email them the link, and they register one in their browser at their next sign-in. A passkey is tied to the host name of the address the tenant signs in at: the server's public address, `ODUDU_PUBLIC_BASE_URL`, or the tenant's domain once it has one. If that host changes, because the tenant switches to a domain or back, passkeys made under the old one are not offered until the address is switched back, and nothing is deleted. If the server's own host changes, they stop working. Browsers offer passkeys only on an https address, or on `localhost`.

## Things that cannot be undone, or that lock people out

Nothing here deletes data, but a flow can lock people out. Requiring a step that people have not set up, or disabling the only step they can pass, leaves them unable to sign in until you change it back. Test a change by signing in with a person of the tenant before you leave the page, and keep a second administrator session open while you do.
