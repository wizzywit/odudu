# Groups in the console

When twenty people in finance need the same access, you do not give it to each of them. You make a group called `finance`, give the group the roles, and put the people in it. Anyone who joins later holds the roles too. This page is for changing who is in a group, what a group carries and where it sits. It needs the `manage-tenant` capability; the menu entry is **Groups** under **IDENTITY**.

## 1. Create a group

Choose **Create a group**. Give it a **Name** and, if you want, a **Description**. The name is fixed once the group exists, because tokens carry the group's path and an application that matches on it would otherwise pass or fail depending on a token's age.

To nest the group, pick a **Parent**. A group with no parent sits at the top level. Then choose **Create group**.

<ConsoleShot
  name="new-group"
  alt="The Create a group form with the name engineering typed in and a parent picker beneath it."
  caption="A group needs a name. It can sit under a parent."
/>

A new group has no members and no roles. The next two steps fill it.

## 2. Give the group roles

Open the group and choose its **Roles** tab. Pick the roles the group carries, then save with **Save Roles**. Every member of the group, and every member of a group beneath it, holds each of these roles from their next token.

## 3. Put people in the group

A group's **Members** tab only lists who belongs. To add someone, open them under [Subjects](./subjects.mdx), choose their **Groups** tab, pick the group and save. A group can also be joined by every new subject automatically: on the **General** tab, under **New subjects**, turn on **Joined by every new subject**. Nobody who already exists is changed by it.

## 4. Move or rename a group

On the **General** tab you can change the **Description**, and the **Parent** under **Place in the tree**. Moving a group changes what its members hold, because they receive the roles of every group above it, so the console asks you to confirm with **Save Place in the tree**. The name never changes. Make a new group instead.

The **Activity** tab lists every change made to the group.

## Things that cannot be undone

- **Delete** on the General tab removes the group and every group beneath it, as **Delete /eng and every group beneath it** for a group at `/eng`. No group beneath it survives as a new top-level group.
- Moving a group, or changing its roles, changes access for every member at once. Check **Members** first.

## Do the same through the API

The calls are in the [groups area of the admin API](../reference/generated/admin-api/groups/index.md). Putting a person in a group is a call on the [subject access area](../reference/generated/admin-api/subject-access/index.md). From the command line, `odudu seed group` and `odudu seed join-group` do the same for a first installation.
