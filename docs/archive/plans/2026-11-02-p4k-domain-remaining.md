# P4k domain 8: the remaining console slices, the key and relay entities, and the shared drain

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:subagent-driven-development. Steps use checkbox syntax.

**Goal:** `settings`, `registration-tokens`, `flow`, `signing-keys`, `email` and `overview` become `pages/`, `failure` becomes `widgets/failure`; what two slices read lives in `entities/signing-key` and `entities/mail-relay`; the services are split by concept, each rule has one owner, the views take their text from use-cases; and what `shared/` still carries that names a domain or sits outside `ui|api|lib|config` is moved or justified, so the closing task can delete the inventory mechanism, with no behaviour changed.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-p4k-frontend-architecture-design.md` (D4, D7, D10, D11). Parent: `2026-11-02-p4k-frontend-architecture.md`, tasks D-A to D-G. ## Global constraints

Those of the domain-7 plan apply unchanged: layer order `app > pages > widgets > features > entities > shared`; entities and shared import no upper layer, an entity imports no entity, shared imports no entity; a feature imports no page, no feature, no widget; a page imports features, widgets and entities, never a page; an index exports only `ui|use-cases|services`, type-only free; a repository or adapter function another slice calls goes through a thin use-case re-export (`entities/subject/use-cases/subjectCalls.ts`); adapters never in an index; use-cases reach adapters only through the repository segment; a service imports an entity index for types only (`entities/area`, `entities/administrator` excepted) and any slice's `types/`; migrated views import no service or repository, and **a use-case phrases nothing and decides nothing** (sentences joined from constants and counts are pure service functions, `spaced()` exists; no no-op bags; a return bag over about 12 fields is grouped per section); files named for concepts, under 300 lines; a new file under `shared/` adds an inventory entry the ratchet refuses, so shared code grows only inside existing files. Every task ends with `pnpm format:check && pnpm lint && pnpm typecheck && pnpm boundaries && pnpm test:unit` plus `pnpm --filter @odudu/docs test`. Dispatches quote the comment rules, the no-`.ts`-extension ruling and the heavy-slot lock; each report carries a "Reuse scan" (behaviour words, `shared/` and every sibling slice). **Behaviour never changes:** a disagreeing copy becomes a named option under "Variants kept", pinned by a test before the copy goes. Fixtures are real transcript bodies, else the documented contract example, said so in the test. Per slice the steps are the parent's D-A (pin), D-B (split), D-C (owner), D-D (adapter), D-E/F (extract), D-G (move, `MIGRATED`), compressed below.

## Rulings

- **Pages:** `settings`, `registration-tokens`, `flow`, `signing-keys`, `email`, `overview` are pages by `git mv` once their services are clean (single consumer each, `router.tsx` only). **`failure` is `widgets/failure`**, not a page: it is a screen of no route, imported by `app/` only, as `session-gate` and `shell` are; alternative rejected: `app/failure` (nothing then holds it to the slice rules). Its index exports `RouteFailure`, `ShellFailure`, `RootBoundary` (ui), `routeTemplate`, `caughtIsLogged`, `CaughtBy` (services) and `recordFailure` (use-cases), as today.
- **Entities `signing-key` and `mail-relay`**, two consumers each (Evidence). `signing-key` = `types/signingKey.ts`, the adapter's `readKeyPage` and the all-pages read `readKeys` (from `overview/adapter.ts:64`, with `MAX_KEY_PAGES`), `services/keyPromotion.ts` (from shared) with `promotionOf` and its `Promotion` kind, `services/keyLane.ts` (the one status vocabulary and `unlisted`/`unknown`), `use-cases/keyCalls.ts`. The signing-keys page keeps stage/promote/retire/delete and the wording (`promotionLabel`, `promotionText`, confirm texts). The list-state `KeyLane` is renamed `KeyLanePages`. `mail-relay` = `types`, `readSmtp` (`shared/adapter/smtp.ts` and its test move in), `services/effective.ts` plus a `hasRelay(config)` that both the email page and the overview's "No mail relay" check read (`effective !== 'none'`); email keeps put/delete/test. Alternatives rejected: both left in `shared/api` (two slices read a record and a rule over it: an entity by D10); one `entities/tenant` extension (a relay is a record of its own).
- **`readDiscovery`** (`shared/adapter/discovery.ts` and test) joins `entities/tenant` (adapter, thin `use-cases` re-export); the overview's `Discovery`/`Jwks` views stay its own.
- **Titles and areas.** `FLOW_TITLE` and `title="Overview"` become a `title` field of the page's use-case, read from `areaAt('flow').label` and `OVERVIEW.label`; the four module-scope `areaAt` in views become hook-supplied. `Place`/`AreaOf` (`overview/services/tiles.ts:23-29`) are a shell concept: `entities/area` exports `placeOf(tenant, path)` (the closure at `useOverview.ts:35-38`: `areaAt` + `areaHref`) and `Place`; `READ_AREA` keeps the read-to-area map and a test asserts `areaAt` accepts every path in it and in `COUNTED`.
- **Lists of names.** One owner in `format.ts`: `listOrNone(items, { join: 'and' | 'comma' })`; `namesOrNone` is `and`, `describeIds`, `describeHoldings`, `describeActions` and the settings `set` are `comma`. Each keeps its text; a characterisation test lists both forms; the UX divergence is a "Variants kept" row with a note for the owner (no text changed here).
- **Number grouping and ranges:** `groupedNumber` replaces the two `toLocaleString` copies (pinned on 1234567 before); `TTL_RULE`'s hand-written "(1 minute)", "(365 days)" use `formatDuration` only if the pinned text is byte-equal, else it stays a Variant; `MINT_LIMITS` is derived from the contracts schema only if it exports the numbers (`grep -n "ttl_seconds" packages/contracts/src/admin`), as `relay.ts` does for the port.
- **Use-case decisions out.** `useGeneralSection`'s `if (disablesTenant(...))` and `useOverview`'s refusal guard and retry fan-out are decisions: `disablesTenant` moves to `entities/tenant/services/tenantEnabled.ts`; `useSaveConfirmation` gains `submitOrAsk(ask | null)` (callers: settings, `useCapabilities`, `useSubjectGroups`, three by grep), the rule returns the ask; the overview's guard and fan-out are pure services and its view model is grouped per panel.
- **Shared drain: one decision per domain-word file** (Task 7), and **the final shared layout** (Task 8): `shared/ui`, `shared/api/{adapter,transport}`, `shared/lib/{services,repository,use-cases}`, no `config` yet. Sub-folders keep every purity and layer rule readable (`console-service-*`, `console-view-no-transport`) with a prefix edit; flattening them into `lib` would erase the line between a pure service and a query hook. The 19 `shared/ui` view entries do not go away by the rename (`view-no-service` matches the `services` segment): the rule gains one stated exemption, "`shared/ui` may import `shared/lib/services`" (the design system's pure formatting helpers), with its fixture, and ADR 0048's Consequences records it.
- **Cross-cutting vocabulary stays in `shared/lib`, named and justified (controller ruling, replaces the `@x` proposal).** The tenant vocabulary (`SYSTEM_TENANT`, `isTenantName`, `tenantPage`, `TENANT_NAME_RULE_TEXT`, `isUnknownTenant`), `principal.ts`'s `Authority`/`AdminCapability` and the capability rules `shared/services/capabilities/*` plus `access.ts` are read by six entities and twelve shared files; using `@x` for them would mean ten or more `@x` files, which the architecture document says signals a wrong boundary, and an entity imports no entity. They are the product's platform vocabulary, not one slice's data. They stay under `shared/lib/services`, listed in ONE place: a small test-checked list `tests/lint/architecture/shared-vocabulary.ts` (file, one-line reason, at most these files, never grown) that `no-domain-in-shared` reads in place of inventory entries, and ADR 0048's Consequences states it. Everything else with a domain word moves to its entity (the rest of Task 7). Cost if wrong: a later phase can move the vocabulary behind `@x` by deleting list entries.
- **Placed out, by name.** Docs-app entries (29 + 23): domain 9. Camel-casing of wire fields and the `contracts-only-in-adapter` sweep: the closing sweep, as in domains 4 to 7. `console-covers-admin-routes` and the gallery are unaffected.
