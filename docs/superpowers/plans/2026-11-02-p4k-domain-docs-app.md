## Rulings

- **Twin owner:** `shotCatalogue({ manifest, directory, noun })` returning `sizeOf(name)` (projects `{width,height}`) and `addressOf(name, scheme)`, with the two thrown texts byte-identical through the `noun` option, and `useShot(catalogue, name)` in `shared/lib/use-cases`. `useConsoleShot(name, priority)` keeps `loadingFor` so `ConsoleShot` imports no service; `usePageShot(name)` is `useShot(pageShots, name)` and stays because it binds the catalogue. Variants kept: none beyond the two options; the size projection is not observable (above). Recorded in the p4k note's "Variants kept" as "none disagree".
- **Landing views take content from `useLandingPage()`**, grouped per section (`capabilities`, `steps`, `readers`, `examples`, `agents`, `branding`, `markdown`, `where`, `pageShots`) and handed down as props; types are type-only imports from services. `useInstall` returns its model grouped `command`, `panels`, `followUp`; `Scene` receives its `SceneModel` from `useSignInFlow` (the hook already owns the step and layout); `DocsHome` and `HomeMap` take `intro` and `sections` from `useDocsHome`. No hook is written for a section that has no state of its own.
- **`classNames` moves to `shared/ui/classNames.ts`** (a presentation helper, four view consumers); its test goes with it. **The `shared/ui` diagram tokens** (`INK`, `SURFACE`, `WIDTH`, `vertical`) stay a service moved to `shared/lib/services/diagram.ts` and rely on domain 8's rule exemption "`shared/ui` may import `shared/lib/services`": Task 6 first confirms by reading `view-no-service.test.ts` that it covers `apps/docs` (it scans both apps), and if the exemption is console-only the same fixture gains a docs row.
- **Decisions out of use-cases:** `revealWanted(capable, reduced)` into `reveal.ts`; `sceneMotionAllowed(reduced, observable)` into `flowMachine.ts`; the `target === null` branch becomes `watchVisible` accepting `null` (returns `undefined`), the unit in `platform`; `copyTimerIdle(status)` into `copyStatus.ts`. `useInstall`'s `WINDOW_TIMERS` `cancel?.()` is **correct** (`Timers.clear` takes `Handle | undefined`, `typewriter.ts:15`): a test pins the `undefined` clear, no code change (the Task 5 minor is closed by that).
